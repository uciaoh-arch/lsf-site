Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-16. Developer: Yu Chiao Huang.

What we collect

speedr does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. It contains no ads, tracking or third-party analytics.

Location

Location is used only on your device to calculate distance and direction to cameras. It never leaves your device. Background location is opt-in and can be turned off any time in iPhone Settings.

Local preferences

Language and alert-distance preferences are stored only on your device.
