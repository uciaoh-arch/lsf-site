Frequently asked questions

The needle never appears. Allow microphone access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → tunertuner, then play one open string near the iPhone. Quiet rooms are best; loud background noise can hide the string.

Which instruments and tunings are supported? Guitar 6 and 7, Bass 4, 5 and 6, four ukulele tunings (GCEA, ADF#B, DGBE, FA#DG), violin (4 and 5 strings), viola, and cello (4 and 5 strings), all in standard tuning. Turn modes on or off from the gear button at the bottom right (each instrument has one switch for all of its tunings); the same page holds the reference pitch and legal links. Guitar 6, Bass 4, Ukulele GCEA and Violin 4 are shown by default.

What does green mean? The string is within ±3 cents of the target note.

Does the app record my audio or need the internet? No. Audio is analyzed live on your iPhone and is never recorded, stored, or uploaded. The app works offline and has no accounts, ads, analytics, or in-app purchases.

Refunds and purchases. Billing and refunds are handled by Apple: https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT204084

Report a problem. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, the tuning mode, and what you played.

常見問題

指針一直不出現。 請到 iOS「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 麥克風 → tunertuner」允許麥克風，然後在 iPhone 附近彈一根空弦。安靜環境最好，過大的背景噪音可能蓋過琴弦。

支援哪些樂器與調弦？ 吉他 6、7 弦，貝斯 4、5、6 弦，四種烏克麗麗調弦（GCEA、ADF#B、DGBE、FA#DG），小提琴（4、5 弦）、中提琴、大提琴（4、5 弦），皆為標準調弦。可從右下角的齒輪開啟或關閉模式（每種樂器有總開關，一鍵全開全關），同一頁也有標準音高與法律資訊；預設顯示吉他 6、貝斯 4、烏克麗麗 GCEA 與小提琴 4。

綠色代表什麼？ 該弦與目標音相差在 ±3 音分以內。

App 會錄音或需要網路嗎？ 不會。聲音只在 iPhone 上即時分析，不錄音、不儲存、不上傳。App 可離線使用，沒有帳號、廣告、數據分析或 App 內購買。
