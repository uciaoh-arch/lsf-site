Privacy Policy — tunertuner

Effective date: 2026-10-19

tunertuner ("the App") is a guitar, bass, ukulele and violin-family tuner for iPhone, developed by Yu Chiao Huang ("we"). This policy explains what the App does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing.

What the App does with the microphone

  • The App asks for microphone access so it can hear the instrument you are tuning.
  • Audio is analyzed in real time, in memory, on your iPhone to find the pitch. It is never recorded, saved to storage, transmitted, or shared. Once a fraction of a second of audio has been analyzed it is discarded.
  • The microphone is only used while the App is open in the foreground, and stops when the App goes to the background.

Information we collect

None. The App has no accounts or sign-in, no backend servers, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting SDKs and no third-party SDKs. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data, device identifiers, usage data, or location.

Information stored on your device

The App stores only three preferences locally using Apple's standard on-device storage: which tuning modes are visible, which mode you last used, and your reference pitch (A4) setting. This data never leaves your device.

Purchases

The App is a paid download sold through the Apple App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we do not receive your payment details or personal information. Apple's own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Children

The App collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can revoke microphone access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → tunertuner. Without it, the App cannot tune.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

隱私權政策 — tunertuner

生效日期：2026-10-19

tunertuner（以下稱「本 App」）是一款 iPhone 吉他、貝斯、烏克麗麗與提琴調音器，由 Yu Chiao Huang（以下稱「我們」）開發。本政策說明本 App 如何處理您的資訊。簡單來說：我們不蒐集任何資料。

麥克風的使用方式

  • 本 App 要求使用麥克風，目的是聽取您正在調音的樂器。
  • 聲音只在您的 iPhone 上、於記憶體中即時分析以判斷音高，不會錄音、不會儲存到裝置、不會傳送或分享。分析完約零點幾秒的聲音後即丟棄。
  • 麥克風只在本 App 於前景開啟時使用；App 進入背景後即停止收音。

我們蒐集的資訊

無。本 App 沒有帳號或登入、沒有後端伺服器、沒有數據分析、沒有廣告、沒有當機回報或任何第三方 SDK。我們不蒐集、儲存、販售或分享任何個人資料、裝置識別碼、使用紀錄或位置資訊。

儲存在您裝置上的資訊

本 App 僅使用 Apple 標準的裝置端儲存方式，在本機保存三項偏好設定：顯示哪些調音模式、上次使用的模式，以及標準音高（A4）設定。這些資料不會離開您的裝置。

購買

本 App 為透過 Apple App Store 販售的付費下載。付款完全由 Apple 處理，我們不會取得您的付款資料或個人資訊。購買行為適用 Apple 自己的隱私權政策。

兒童

本 App 不蒐集任何人的資料，包括兒童。

您的選擇

您可以隨時在 iOS「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 麥克風 → tunertuner」關閉麥克風權限。關閉後 App 將無法調音。

政策變更
